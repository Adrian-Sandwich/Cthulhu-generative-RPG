An ancient lighthouse on a rocky shore. The beam rotates endlessly, calling to something in the deep.

HOW TO PLAY

A text adventure. You type what your investigator does in plain words — look around, read the keeper's log, go inside — and the Keeper narrates what happens.

When an action is risky, you'll roll dice. Roll under your skill to succeed. Horror erodes your SAN; injury drains HP. Reach zero and your story ends.

Pick an archetype below — its stats and skills shape what you're good at. Then name your investigator and begin.

HOW TO PLAY [close]

The goal. Investigate the lighthouse, survive, and uncover what happened to its keeper. Every playthrough is different — the Keeper (an AI) narrates your story.

Playing. Type what you do in the > what do you do? box in plain language. Examples:

  • examine the door
  • climb the stairs to the top
  • talk to the lieutenant about the logs

Dice. Risky actions trigger a roll. A pixel die appears with a target number — click it (or press space) to throw. Roll under the target to succeed. The Keeper then narrates the outcome.

Your investigator.

  • HP — health. Hits zero, you die.
  • SAN — sanity. Cosmic horror chips away at it; low SAN brings madness.
  • LUCK — fortune that can swing close calls.

Buttons.

  • [help] — this screen.
  • [sheet] — full stats and skills.
  • [history] — replay everything so far.
  • [new game] — start over with a new investigator.
